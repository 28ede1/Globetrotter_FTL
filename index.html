Discover Williamstown

Exploring the Purple Valley

Experience what life is like at Williams College, tucked away in the middle of nowhere in the beautiful Berkshires of Western Massachusetts. Surrounded by mountains, forests, and quiet New England charm, the campus feels both peaceful and remote. Life in the Purple Valley moves at its own pace, where everything is within walking distance, nature is always close by, and the tight-knit community makes even a small place feel full of life. It’s a setting where the landscape and the college community are deeply connected, shaping a quite unique student experience.
