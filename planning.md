1) What location are you building this guide for, and why did you choose it?

I want to have the project be about my college town. I go to Williams College and I want to share what life is like to be a student at my school. For the purposes of this project, having a lot of background information on the location will make it easier for me to figure out what relevant content should be found on each page.

2) Who is your primary audience? (e.g., first-time visitors, backpackers, families, local residents)

Friends at other schools who want to know about what makes Williams College special. 

3) What do you want visitors to feel or know after spending 5 minutes on your site?

I want the visitors to be surpised by what they learn about the school and to  have a better understanding for what my experiences have been like so far at Williams College. 

4) What's one design decision — color, layout, or tone — that reflects your destination's identity?

I want the website colors and tone to match the school spirit and vibe of Williams College.
